About us

Gateway Poly Pack

A packaging supplier based in Sardarnagar, Ahmedabad, built around one aim: your gateway to better packaging.

We deal in ABL and PBL laminates and supply laminated tubes, stand-up caps, cartons and packaging material to brands in oral care, pharma, cosmetics, industrial and food products.

Our standard tubes come in eight diameters from 16 to 35 mm. If you need a different size, length or print, tell us and we will work out what is possible.
